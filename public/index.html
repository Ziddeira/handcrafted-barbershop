The Shop

Old-school craft.
Fresh-cut results.

Handcrafted is a neighborhood barbershop built on one idea: every cut deserves full attention. No rushing, no cookie-cutter fades — just clean blends, sharp lines and a finish that grows out right.

Dads bring their kids, first-timers become regulars, and last-minute appointments get squeezed in whenever we can. Sit back in the chair and let the clippers do the talking.

  • Detail-obsessed barbers, including our go-to, Eddie
  • Kids and families always welcome
  • Easy online booking, any time, with Booksy
